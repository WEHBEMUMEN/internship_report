1 & \textbf{Cox-de Boor Basis} & $N_{i,p}(u) = \frac{u - u_i}{u_{i+p} - u_i} N_{i,p-1} + \frac{u_{i+p+1} - u}{u_{i+p+1} - u_{i+1}} N_{i+1,p-1}$ & $u, p, \Xi$: Parametric coordinate $u$, degree $p$, knot vector $\Xi$. Determines local support $[u_i, u_{i+p+1})$ and $C^{p-k}$ continuity. & \texttt{app/shared/nurbs-core.js} (\texttt{basisFuns}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} (\texttt{basis\_funs}) \\
\hline

2 & \textbf{Basis Derivative} & $N'_{i,p}(u) = \frac{p}{u_{i+p}-u_i}N_{i,p-1} - \frac{p}{u_{i+p+1}-u_{i+1}}N_{i+1,p-1}$ & $p, \Delta u$: Inverse knot span length scales gradient magnitude. Degree $p$ reduces to $p-1$. & \texttt{app/shared/nurbs-core.js} (\texttt{dersBasisFuns}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} (\texttt{ders\_basis\_funs}) \\
\hline

3 & \textbf{NURBS Shape Functions} & $R_{i,j}^{p,q}(u,v) = \frac{N_{i,p}(u) M_{j,q}(v) w_{i,j}}{\sum N_{k,p} M_{l,q} w_{k,l}}$ & $w_{i,j}$: Control point weights. Enables exact representation of circular arcs (e.g. notch boundary) via rational projection. & \texttt{app/shared/nurbs-core.js} (\texttt{evalNURBS}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} \\
\hline

4 & \textbf{Geometric Mapping} & $\mathbf{x}(u,v) = \sum R_{i,j}^{p,q}(u,v) \mathbf{P}_{i,j}$ & $\mathbf{P}_{i,j}$: Control point coordinates. Maps unit parametric square $[0,1]^2$ to physical beam geometry $\Omega$. & \texttt{app/shared/nurbs-core.js} (\texttt{evalSurfacePoint}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} \\
\hline

5 & \textbf{Jacobian Matrix} & $\mathbf{J} = \begin{bmatrix} \partial_u x & \partial_u y \\ \partial_v x & \partial_v y \end{bmatrix}, \quad J = \det \mathbf{J}$ & $\partial_{\xi} R_a, \mathbf{P}_a$: Measures local physical area expansion $dx\,dy = J\,du\,dv$. Invertibility requires $J > 0$. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{computeJacobian}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} \\
\hline

6 & \textbf{Physical Derivatives} & $\begin{bmatrix} \partial_x R_a \\ \partial_y R_a \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{J}^{-1} \begin{bmatrix} \partial_u R_a \\ \partial_v R_a \end{bmatrix}$ & $\mathbf{J}^{-1}$: Pulls back physical PDE gradient operators back to static reference parameter space. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{computeDerivatives}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} \\
\hline

7 & \textbf{Elasticity Tensor $\mathbf{D}$} & $\mathbf{D} = \frac{E}{1-\nu^2}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & \nu & 0 \\ \nu & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \frac{1-\nu}{2} \end{bmatrix}$ & $E, \nu$: Young's modulus scales stiffness linearly; Poisson's ratio couples axial and lateral strains. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{assembleStiffness}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} \\
\hline

8 & \textbf{Strain-Displacement $\mathbf{B}$} & $\mathbf{B}_a = \begin{bmatrix} \partial_x R_a & 0 \\ 0 & \partial_y R_a \\ \partial_y R_a & \partial_x R_a \end{bmatrix}$ & $\partial_x R_a, \partial_y R_a$: Relates control node displacements to physical strain components $\boldsymbol{\varepsilon} = \mathbf{B} \mathbf{d}$. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{buildBMatrix}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} \\
\hline

9 & \textbf{Element Stiffness $\mathbf{K}_e$} & $\mathbf{K}_e = \iint \mathbf{B}^T \mathbf{D} \mathbf{B} \, J \, du \, dv$ & $\mathbf{B}, \mathbf{D}, J$: Evaluated at $(p+1)(q+1)$ Gauss points for exact integration without numerical locking. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{assembleElementK}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} \\
\hline

10 & \textbf{Element Mass $\mathbf{M}_e$} & $\mathbf{M}_e = \rho t_h \iint \mathbf{R}^T \mathbf{R} \, J \, du \, dv$ & $\rho, t_h$: Mass density and beam thickness. Ensures exact kinetic energy representation. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{assembleElementM}), \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py} \\
\hline

11 & \textbf{Shifted Pullback $\boldsymbol{\Psi}$} & $\mathbf{x}(\boldsymbol{\xi}; \boldsymbol{\mu}) = \hat{\mathbf{x}}(\boldsymbol{\xi}) + \mathbf{v}_{\text{map}}(\boldsymbol{\xi}; \boldsymbol{\mu})$ & $\boldsymbol{\mu} = (r, x_c)^T$: Maps parameterized physical domain $\Omega(\boldsymbol{\mu})$ to static reference domain $\hat{\Omega}$, unifying vector spaces for POD. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{updateGeometry}), \texttt{iga.js} \\
\hline

12 & \textbf{Rayleigh Damping} & $\mathbf{C} = \alpha_m \mathbf{M} + \beta_k \mathbf{K}$ & $\alpha_m, \beta_k$: Proportional damping coefficients for low-frequency and high-frequency structural modes. & \texttt{physics.js} (\texttt{buildDampingMatrix}) \\
\hline

13 & \textbf{Generalized-$\alpha$ Params} & $\alpha_m = \frac{2\rho_\infty-1}{\rho_\infty+1}, \alpha_f = \frac{\rho_\infty}{\rho_\infty+1}$ & $\rho_\infty \in [0, 1]$: Spectral radius controlling high-frequency numerical dissipation without degrading accuracy. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{initAlphaParams}), \texttt{physics.js} \\
\hline

14 & \textbf{Tangent Matrix $\mathbf{K}_{\text{eff}}$} & $\mathbf{K}_{\text{eff}} = (1-\alpha_m)\mathbf{M} + (1-\alpha_f)\gamma\Delta t\mathbf{C} + (1-\alpha_f)\beta\Delta t^2\mathbf{K}$ & $\Delta t, \beta, \gamma$: Effective stiffness matrix solved at each time step for acceleration $\ddot{\mathbf{u}}_{n+1}$. & \texttt{app/shared/fom-solver.js} (\texttt{stepTimeIntegration}) \\
\hline

15 & \textbf{SVD / POD Extraction} & $\mathbf{S} = \mathbf{U} \boldsymbol{\Sigma} \mathbf{V}^T \implies \boldsymbol{\Phi} = \mathbf{U}_{:, 1:r}$ & $\mathbf{S}, r, \eta$: Snapshot matrix $\mathbf{S}$. $r$ chosen where cumulative energy ratio $\eta(r) \ge 99.99\%$. & \texttt{visualizer/get\_pod\_singular\_values.py}, \texttt{rom-engine.js} \\
\hline

16 & \textbf{Galerkin ROM} & $\mathbf{M}_r \ddot{\mathbf{q}} + \mathbf{C}_r \dot{\mathbf{q}} + \mathbf{K}_r(\boldsymbol{\mu}) \mathbf{q} = \mathbf{F}_r$ & $\boldsymbol{\Phi}$: Projects $N$-dimensional system down to $r$ reduced coordinates $\mathbf{q}(t)$. & \texttt{rom-engine.js} (\texttt{solveROM}) \\
\hline

17 & \textbf{ECSW Hyper-Reduction} & $\mathbf{K}_{r,\text{ECSW}} = \sum_{e \in \Omega_{\text{ECSW}}} w_e \boldsymbol{\Phi}_e^T \mathbf{K}_e \boldsymbol{\Phi}_e$ & $w_e > 0, \Omega_{\text{ECSW}}$: Non-negative element weights via NNLS. Replaces full assembly to achieve \textbf{39x speedup} (0.90 ms vs 35.10 ms). & \texttt{visualizer/mesh\_convergence.py}, \texttt{rom-engine.js} \\
