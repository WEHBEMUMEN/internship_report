\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript entitled \textbf{"Projection-based Reduced Order Modeling for Structural Dynamics via Isogeometric Analysis and Hyper-Reduction"} for publication consideration as a regular research article in \textit{Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering}.

In this manuscript, we present an integrated computational framework combining Isogeometric Analysis (IGA) with reference domain mapping and projection-based Reduced Order Modeling (pROM) via hyper-reduction for parameterized transient structural dynamics. By exploiting the exact geometry representation of B-splines and NURBS, parameter-dependent physical geometries are pulled back to a fixed, parameter-independent reference parametric domain $\hat{\Omega} = (0,1)^d$. This preserves consistent control-point degrees of freedom without remeshing or spatial interpolation across shape variations. Furthermore, Energy-Conserving Sampling and Weighting (ECSW) hyper-reduction is formulated to resolve the non-affine Jacobian evaluation bottleneck, slashing element assembly by over $99\%$ and achieving speedup factors from $64.5\times$ up to $386\times$ on 2D and 3D elastodynamic benchmarks with relative errors bounded below $0.06\%$.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not currently under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the manuscript.

Thank you for considering our work for publication in \textit{Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering}.

\closing{Sincerely,}
