\opening{Dear Editors-in-Chief,}

We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript entitled \textbf{"Projection-based Reduced Order Modeling for Structural Dynamics via Isogeometric Analysis and Hyper-Reduction"} for publication consideration as a regular research article in \textit{Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering}.

In this manuscript, we present an integrated computational framework combining Isogeometric Analysis (IGA) with reference configuration mapping and projection-based Reduced Order Modeling (pROM) accelerated via hyper-reduction for parameterized transient structural dynamics. By exploiting the exact spline geometry representation of B-splines and NURBS, parameter-dependent physical configurations $\Omega(\bm{\mu})$ are pulled back to an invariant reference domain $\hat{\Omega}$. This preserves consistent control-point degrees of freedom and an identical algebraic index space without spatial remeshing or re-interpolation across shape variations. Furthermore, Energy-Conserving Sampling and Weighting (ECSW) hyper-reduction is formulated for IGA discretizations to resolve the non-affine Jacobian evaluation bottleneck for both stiffness and mass operators, slashing element assembly by over $99.1\%$ and achieving speedup factors of $64.5\times$ on 2D parameterized elastodynamics and $386\times$ on 3D high-order dynamic benchmarks with relative $L_2$ errors bounded below $0.054\%$.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not currently under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the manuscript.
