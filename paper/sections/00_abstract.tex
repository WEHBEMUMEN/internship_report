Parameterized structural dynamics often demands repeated solution of the equations of motion across varying geometric configurations and operating conditions. Traditional numerical approaches rely on Finite Element methods where shape variations necessitate frequent remeshing and projection across incompatible meshes. This paper presents an integrated framework combining Isogeometric Analysis (IGA) with Projection-based Reduced Order Modeling (pROM) and hyper-reduction for structural dynamics. By exploiting the exact geometry description offered by B-splines and Non-Uniform Rational B-Splines (NURBS), parameter-dependent physical geometries are mapped back to a parameter-independent reference parametric domain. This eliminates the requirement for spatial remeshing and ensures consistent algebraic degrees of freedom across the parameter space. Proper Orthogonal Decomposition (POD) and Galerkin projection yield a drastically reduced-order system of dynamic equations. To address the computational bottleneck introduced by non-affine parameter-dependent metric tensors and geometric mappings, hyper-reduction is applied to decouple online evaluation complexity from the full-order mesh dimension. Numerical verification on representative 2D and 3D transient elastodynamic benchmarks demonstrates substantial dimensionality reduction, computational acceleration, and high accuracy.
