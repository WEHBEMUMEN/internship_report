In this paper, we presented an integrated computational framework combining Isogeometric Analysis (IGA), reference configuration mapping, and projection-based Reduced Order Modeling (pROM) accelerated via hyper-reduction for parameterized transient structural dynamics.

By leveraging the exact spline geometry representation and the smooth parametric mappings of NURBS, parameter-dependent physical configurations $\Omega(\bm{\mu})$ are pulled back to an invariant reference domain $\hat{\Omega}$. This fundamental shift circumvents the need for spatial remeshing, mesh morphing, or coordinate re-interpolation across geometric shape variations, ensuring that dynamic snapshot vectors share an identical algebraic degree-of-freedom space $\mathbb{R}^{N_{\text{dof}}}$. Dimensionality reduction via Proper Orthogonal Decomposition (POD) and Galerkin projection captures over $99.999\%$ of snapshot energy within low-dimensional subspaces ($r \le 15$).

To eliminate the severe non-affine computational bottleneck induced by parameter-dependent Jacobians and metric tensors, Energy-Conserving Sampling and Weighting (ECSW) hyper-reduction was formulated and tailored to IGA discretizations. By solving a constrained Non-Negative Least Squares (NNLS) optimization problem with coercivity pre-seeding at boundary and stress-concentration features, both stiffness and mass operator assemblies are restricted to a minimal active subset of knot spans ($N_e^* = 28$ out of $3,200$, a $99.12\%$ sparsity reduction). This decoupling renders online evaluation complexity completely independent of the full-order mesh dimension $N_{\text{dof}}$, achieving a $64.5\times$ speedup on the 2D parameterized notched benchmark with relative $L_2$ errors bounded below $0.054\%$, while large-scale 3D $C^2$ trivariate B-spline reduction delivered a $386\times$ computational acceleration.

Despite these compelling advantages, certain limitations warrant consideration. The reference mapping paradigm relies on the existence of an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism with positive Jacobian determinant ($J > 0$); hence, severe topological transitions—such as crack propagation, hole nucleation, or severe self-contact—cannot be represented within a single-patch coordinate pullback and require multi-patch domain decomposition or enriched partition-of-unity methods. Furthermore, while the online simulation achieves sub-millisecond solve times, the offline training phase involves snapshot generation and NNLS optimization, making the approach best suited for many-query scenarios such as sensitivity analysis, design optimization, and real-time interactive digital twins.

Future research will focus on extending the hyper-reduced IGA formulation to finite-strain geometrically and materially nonlinear elastodynamics, multi-patch trimmed geometries, and client-side digital twin architectures for cyber-physical structural health monitoring.
