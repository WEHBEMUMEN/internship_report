\item Proper Orthogonal Decomposition (POD) basis extraction for structural dynamics reduction.
\item Real-time web-based 3D digital twin visualizer executing online ROM states at $>$24 FPS.
\item Order-of-magnitude reduction in degrees of freedom with strict energy preservation.
\end{highlights}
